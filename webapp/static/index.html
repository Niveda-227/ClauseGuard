    How it works

    Three steps, all on this computer.

    1. Split into sentences

      The text is cut into sentences, and each sentence keeps its exact position so it can be highlighted in place.

    2. Score every sentence eight times

      A classifier trained on 5,532 sentences from 30 real Terms of Service scores each sentence once per category. A sentence is flagged when a score passes that category's cut-off.

    3. You read the original words

      Flags send you to sentences worth reading. The model makes mistakes in both directions, so the decision stays with you.

    Checked on 2,275 sentences from 10 companies the model never saw in training: macro-F1 0.68 (leave-one-company-out estimate). It still misses some clauses and mislabels others. Details are in docs/MODEL_CARD.md.
